About me

I’m Bharath Kumar, a Principal Product Designer focused on building scalable digital products and the teams behind them.
‍
My design principles are rooted in clarity, systems thinking, and thoughtful execution that drives real outcomes. I bring structure to complexity, align product direction with business goals, and turn layered systems into experiences that feel simple and intentional.
‍
I care about my work, but I care just as much about the people building it alongside me and the people behind the screen using it.
